FAQ

Do you build standalone AI demos?

We focus on AI that sits inside your product or operations. Standalone demos without a path to production are out of scope.

Which models and vendors do you use?

We choose based on constraints: latency, cost, data sensitivity, and existing stack. The recommendation is part of scoping, not a fixed vendor pitch.

Can AI work be part of an MVP?

Yes, when it removes real friction in v1. If it is optional theater, we say so and keep it out of the first release.

Next step
