Product studio vs freelance vs offshore agency

A plain comparison of how startups usually buy software help. Use it to match engagement shape to risk, not to chase the lowest day rate.

Book a discovery call

Freelance developer

  • Fits — narrow, well-specified tasks; you already have technical leadership
  • Strength — speed to start, flexible hours, lower fixed overhead
  • Risk — bus factor, uneven coverage across mobile/backend/ops, scope creep without a product owner

Offshore agency

  • Fits — larger volume of build work with a local product owner who can specify tightly
  • Strength — capacity and process templates at scale
  • Risk — time zones, diluted accountability, specs that look complete but miss product judgment

Product engineering studio

  • Fits — MVP or modernization where architecture and delivery must move together
  • Strength — coordinated surfaces, senior review, written scope and milestones
  • Risk — higher rate than a solo freelancer; you still must own product decisions

Decision checklist

  • Do you already have someone who can set architecture and accept work?
  • How many disciplines does v1 need at once?
  • Is the cost of a wrong build higher than the savings on rate?
  • Will you operate the system after launch, and who documents that?

FAQ

Which option is cheapest?

A single freelancer often has the lowest sticker rate. Total cost depends on rework, coordination time, and whether you still need architecture and QA coverage.

When does a studio make sense?

When you need several disciplines coordinated (mobile, backend, release), want senior judgment in the loop, and prefer a bounded written scope over assembling freelancers yourself.

Is Zinova a studio or a staffing firm?

Zinova is a product engineering company. Engagements start with discovery, then a written proposal. We do not sell anonymous bench staffing through the website.
