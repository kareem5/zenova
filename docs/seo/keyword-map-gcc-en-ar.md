## Country mentions (body / FAQ, not every title)

Legal line stays Estonia (`Zinova OÜ`). Do not invent local offices or phone numbers.

## Content rules

- One primary intent per URL; EN/AR as separate indexable URLs with hreflang.
- Titles: query first, brand last (~50–60 characters).
- Prefer natural Gulf/GCC mentions in H2/FAQ over keyword stuffing.
- No `/locations/...` pages until Search Console shows country-level demand.

## GSC checklist (manual after deploy)

1. Verify URL-prefix property `https://www.zinova.io/`.
2. Resubmit `https://www.zinova.io/sitemap.xml`.
3. Request indexing once for `/`, `/ar/`, MVP pages, and `/guides/mvp-for-gcc-startups.html`.
4. After ~4 weeks, filter Queries for: GCC, Gulf, Saudi, UAE, السعودية، الإمارات، الخليج.
5. Only then consider dedicated country landing pages for queries with sustained impressions.
