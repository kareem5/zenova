How Zinova delivery typically looks

An honest snapshot of markets we serve, how engagements are shaped, and how success is measured — not a public client case study, and not invented results.

This page is a delivery overview. It does not name clients, display logos, claim endorsements, or invent anonymized outcomes. Former employers’ IP and commercial results are never claimed as Zinova’s.

Markets we serve

Zinova OÜ is registered in Estonia and works remote-first with:

  • Gulf founders and product teams across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman
  • European startups — especially Estonia and nearby Nordics/Baltics (Finland, Latvia, Lithuania, Sweden) and nearby EU product teams

Regional constraints (Arabic/RTL, Gulf payments, GDPR, EU rails) live in written scope — not in doorway pages.

Engagement shapes

Most work falls into one of these shapes, sometimes sequenced:

  • Discovery & roadmap — lock product intent, boundaries, architecture direction, and a written plan before a full build
  • Bounded build — MVP or staged release with clear ownership from scope to ship (mobile, web, backend, QA, release support)
  • Technical audit — architecture, performance, and release reliability with actionable owners — not a long report with no next step
  • Consulting / oversight — senior judgment between CTO hires: roadmaps, vendor review, delivery shape
  • AI in product — scoped features that reduce friction in production workflows, not standalone demos

Stack themes

Judgment shows up where delivery is hardest. Themes we work in (aligned with our capabilities, not a client roster):

  • Architecture and platform boundaries that stay coherent as traffic and teams grow
  • CI/CD and release engineering that reduce production variance
  • Product-grade iOS alongside dependable APIs and data paths
  • Subscriptions, entitlements, and payments-adjacent lifecycle flows
  • Backends under real operational load — latency, failure modes, observability
  • Pragmatic AI features anchored in user workflows

How success is measured

We measure engagements against decisions and ownership — not vanity KPIs:

  • A written scope both sides can estimate against
  • Milestones that ship vertical slices (a user can complete a job)
  • Release readiness: checks, ownership of environments, and a clear handoff
  • The next founder decision unlocked (pilot, fundraise proof, rewrite vs harden, hire vs partner)

Example engagement outline

Template for a future approved anonymized story — structure only, no invented client or metrics:

  • Problem — what decision or bottleneck the engagement had to unlock
  • Approach — discovery, build, audit, or consulting shape; what stayed out of scope
  • Deliverables — artefacts and surfaces shipped (roadmap, release, audit findings with owners)
  • Next decision — what the founder could decide or do after handoff
