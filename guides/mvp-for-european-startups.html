What “MVP” should mean in Estonia and the Nordics

For European startups, a useful MVP unlocks a concrete next decision: a pilot customer in Tallinn or Helsinki, a fundraising proof point, or an operational bottleneck removed. It is not a smaller version of the entire vision, and it is not a list of every EU compliance feature you might need later.

Scope before you hire

  • One sentence for the decision v1 must unlock
  • Three to seven capabilities; explicit non-goals
  • GDPR stance: what personal data v1 processes, and who is controller vs processor
  • Platforms, EU payments path, and who owns store accounts
  • Who accepts work daily (founder, product lead, or both)

Use the scoping checklist before a studio or freelancer starts billing.

Regional product realities

  • GDPR & privacy — name the data and roles in scope; do not bolt policy on after launch
  • EU payments & subscriptions — name the rails and stores you can actually open now
  • Store accounts — App Store / Play EU ownership and listing copy are product work
  • Timezone — Nordics and Baltics overlap well with an Estonia-registered remote partner
