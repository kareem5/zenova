How to scope an MVP before you hire

A practical checklist for founders: what belongs in v1, what to cut, and what to write down before a studio, freelancer, or agency starts billing.

Book a discovery call

Start with the decision the MVP must unlock

An MVP is not a smaller version of the dream product. It is the smallest release that lets you learn or sell something specific: a pilot customer, a fundraising proof point, or an operational bottleneck removed. Write that decision in one sentence before listing features.

Define boundaries and non-goals

  • Who the first users are (and who they are not)
  • The three to seven capabilities required for that decision
  • Explicit non-goals: admin suites, every integration, multi-region, white-label
  • Platforms in v1: iOS, Android, web, or a subset

Sequence milestones, not a wish list

Break work into slices that can ship and be tested. Prefer vertical slices (a user can complete a job) over horizontal layers (UI only, then API only). Note dependencies: payments, auth providers, app store accounts, legal copy.

Capture technical constraints early

  • Data sensitivity and where systems must live
  • Must-use vendors (payments, identity, messaging)
  • Team that will operate the product after launch
  • Budget and calendar you will not move

What to bring to a partner conversation

  • One-page problem and user description
  • v1 feature list with non-goals
  • Rough success metric for the first release
  • Known constraints and open questions

FAQ

How detailed should an MVP brief be?

Enough that two engineers would estimate the same surfaces. User, problem, v1 features, non-goals, platforms, and constraints on budget and date.

Should we write technical specs ourselves?

Not always. You should own product intent and constraints. Architecture depth can come from discovery with a partner if you lack that skill in-house.

When is Zinova a fit after scoping?

When you want senior-led discovery or a bounded build with written scope. Start with a discovery call; there is no automatic purchase on the site.
