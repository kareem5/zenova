MVP development for startups

Zinova builds launch-ready MVPs: mobile and web frontends, backend and APIs, payments and subscriptions when they matter, plus QA and release support. Structured communication and engineering judgment matched to your stage.

Book a discovery call

Who this is for

  • Teams ready to move from discovery into a bounded build
  • Startups that need a credible v1 without overbuilding
  • Organizations replacing a prototype or spreadsheet-led workflow with production software
  • Companies adding a new surface (mobile app, partner API, billing) with clear acceptance criteria

Built for Gulf and European product teams

Founders across the Gulf and Europe — including Estonia, Finland, Latvia, Lithuania, and Sweden — often need a bounded v1 without overbuilding. Gulf teams may need Arabic UX and regional payments; European teams often need GDPR-aware scope and EU rails, with remote collaboration and Nordics/Baltics timezone overlap.

What’s included

  • Frontend & backend development — mobile and/or web aligned to scope
  • APIs & integrations — auth, data models, third-party services as needed
  • QA & release support — checks and cadence suited to your timeline
  • Scalable foundations — sensible defaults for growth without premature complexity
  • AI in workflows — where it removes friction; skipped where it adds noise

How we work

  1. Scope & milestones — explicit boundaries and acceptance criteria.
  2. Build — iterative delivery with steady visibility.
  3. Stabilize — harden for launch; observe what breaks first.
  4. Release & transition — handoff your team can operate.

What is out of scope

  • Open-ended retainer work with no written milestones
  • Building every feature on a brainstorm list before the first release
  • Guaranteeing market traction; we own technical delivery, you own product-market decisions
  • Taking over legal, payroll, or fundraising work

FAQ

How long does an MVP build usually take?

Most bounded MVP builds land in a multi-week to few-month window once scope and acceptance criteria are written. Timeline depends on surfaces (mobile, web, backend), integrations, and how much discovery is already done.

Do you build both the frontend and the backend?

Yes. Zinova delivers mobile and web frontends together with backend APIs, auth, and integrations when they are in scope. The written proposal states which surfaces are included.

What happens after launch?

We stabilize the release, document how to operate it, and can continue with improvements if you want an ongoing engagement. Handoff is part of the plan, not an afterthought.
