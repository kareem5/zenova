Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Zinova handles information in connection with this website. It is provided for transparency and does not constitute legal advice or an exhaustive statement of every obligation that may apply.

Last updated: May 2026

Introduction

Zinova OÜ (“Zinova”, “we”, “us”) operates this website as a simple, static presence for information and inquiries. We aim to collect only what is reasonable for responding to leads and maintaining the site.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

  • Information you send us, such as name, email address, company name, and the content of your message when you use the contact flow or email us directly.
  • Technical information that browsers and hosting infrastructure commonly generate when pages are loaded, such as IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser type, device type, and timestamps.

How we use your information

We use this information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule introductory conversations.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Contact forms and email communication

The contact experience on this site uses your email client (for example, via a mailto link). Messages are transmitted based on your device and email provider settings. We do not operate an integrated inbox or payment flow on this website.

Analytics and cookies

This site is built as lightweight static HTML. If analytics tools are used in the future, they would be configured to respect applicable requirements and this policy would be updated accordingly. Essential cookies or similar technologies may be used by hosting or infrastructure providers as part of delivering the site.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who assist us in hosting, email, or IT operations, and when required by law or to protect our legitimate interests in a proportionate way.

Data retention

We retain inquiry-related information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, including managing client relationships and legal record-keeping, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority where applicable.
