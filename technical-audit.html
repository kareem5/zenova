Who this is for

  • Teams whose releases feel risky or slow
  • Products under load with unclear bottlenecks
  • Founders inheriting a codebase before hiring or fundraising
  • Companies planning a rewrite and wanting evidence first

For products serving Gulf users

Teams in or selling into Saudi Arabia, the UAE, and the wider GCC use audits when releases feel risky, performance dips under load, or a rewrite decision needs evidence first.

What’s included

  • Architecture review — service boundaries, ownership, and coupling risks
  • Performance & reliability — where latency and failure modes show up
  • Release engineering — pipelines, checks, and deployment cadence
  • Debt map — what to fix now vs. what can wait
  • Action plan — prioritized recommendations with clear owners

How we work

  1. Brief — goals, constraints, and systems in scope.
  2. Inspect — code, architecture, ops signals, and release process.
  3. Synthesize — findings ranked by risk and cost to fix.
  4. Hand off — written recommendations your team can execute.

FAQ

What do you review in a technical audit?

Architecture boundaries, performance hotspots, release and CI/CD health, reliability risks, and debt that blocks delivery. Depth follows your goals and the systems you open for review.

Will we get a long report with no owners?

No. Deliverables emphasize prioritized findings, impact, and suggested next moves your engineers or Zinova can take.

Can this lead into a build or consulting engagement?

Yes, when it fits. Audit and follow-on work stay separate written proposals so scope stays clear.
