What’s included

  • Architecture & roadmaps — decisions tied to stage and constraints

  • Delivery oversight — cadence, quality bar, and risk visibility
  • Product-technical alignment — keeping scope honest as the product moves

How we work

  1. Define — decisions you need and the cadence that fits.
  2. Engage — working sessions, reviews, and written recommendations.
  3. Steer — keep delivery and architecture aligned as reality changes.

FAQ

Is this a fractional CTO engagement?

It can look similar in practice: senior technical leadership on a defined cadence. We name scope in a written proposal rather than selling a vague title.

Do you also write code?

When the engagement calls for it, yes. Many consulting engagements stay at architecture, roadmap, and oversight; others mix hands-on delivery. The proposal states which.

How do engagements usually start?

A short discovery call, then a written proposal with scope, cadence, and commercial terms. No automatic purchase through the website.

Next step

Tell us what is breaking, what you want to ship, and any hard constraints. We’ll confirm fit and propose a clear next step.
